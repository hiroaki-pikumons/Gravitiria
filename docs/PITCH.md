# Gravitiria: The Short Version

Text you can paste into a store page, a Unity Play listing, a post, or an email. Pick the length you need.

## One line

Look at a wall. Let go. Now it's the floor.

## One paragraph

Gravitiria is a first-person gravity puzzle. Hold the aim button and time slows to a crawl; release it and gravity snaps toward whatever you were looking at. Eleven rooms turn that single idea into a climb up a laser-lined tower, a chase across the underside of a floor, and a final gauntlet where the enemies fall the same way you do. There is almost no text. Colour, light and the shape of the room tell you what to try next. The timer never forgets a death, so a clean run is worth bragging about.

## Three paragraphs

Every stage in Gravitiria is a room you cannot cross on foot. The way out is on a wall, or behind the floor, or across a ceiling striped with lasers. You get one tool: hold to slow time and aim, release to make the surface you are looking at the new "down". Your body swings, the room rotates around you, and what was a wall is now a corridor.

The first five stages teach the vocabulary: the underside of a floor, walls as roads, jump pads over laser lines, blue fields that slow everything, and sentinels that speed up when you slow time. The five Remix stages take the training wheels off. No hints, no messages. A switch and the lasers it controls share a colour. A patrol drone that falls with your gravity can be dropped onto a button. A dash with a sliver of invincibility opens a rhythm of blinking beams.

Gravitiria plays with a keyboard and mouse, a gamepad, or on a phone with one thumb on a stick and the other on FLIP. It speaks Japanese and English, remembers your best times and your unlocked moves, and ends with a tally of every stage in your run. It is short on purpose: one sitting to see the ending, and a reason to come back for the time.

## Feature bullets

- A single mechanic, explored across 11 stages
- Slow-motion aiming that makes every flip deliberate
- Enemies that obey your gravity and die to the same lasers you do
- Two unlockable moves: Dash (with i-frames) and a four-way quick flip
- Wordless Remix stages guided by colour and light
- A run timer and death counter that survive respawns, plus best-time records
- Japanese and English, switchable on the title screen
- Keyboard, gamepad and touch; Windows, Android and browser builds
